Track your portfolio with clarity

PSX Folio is a mobile-first portfolio tracker for PSX investors. It helps you record trades, monitor holdings, and view performance in one place.

What PSX Folio does

  • Track buy/sell transactions
  • Monitor portfolio performance and free cash
  • Manage watchlists and market index views
  • Work with offline-first local data

Data handling

PSX Folio does not collect your personal portfolio records on our servers. Your entries are stored locally on your device.

The app fetches public PSX-related market data directly to your device for live values. Read the Privacy Policy for complete details.
